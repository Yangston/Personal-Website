## A template, not a finished trip

This draft demonstrates how a collection can combine structured media with a written field note. Replace these temporary frames with immutable Vercel Blob URLs, update their dimensions and descriptions, and choose the layout rhythm that fits the work.

The presentation belongs to the collection—not to a universal gallery template. A quiet landscape sequence can remain spare, while a city collection can become dense, kinetic, and typographic.
