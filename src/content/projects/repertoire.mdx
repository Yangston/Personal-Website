## Planning without friction

Repertoire organized more than fifty exercises by muscle group and difficulty, then let users assemble them into repeatable schedules.

The project was an early exercise in separating domain rules from interface details. Its next iteration would focus less on feature count and more on reducing the time between opening the app and starting a workout.
