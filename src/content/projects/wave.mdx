## A hand becomes the controller

Wave explored whether a small vocabulary of hand signs could replace common mouse and keyboard actions. The system combined a camera pipeline, landmark extraction, and a feed-forward classifier trained across more than twenty gestures.

The original prototype reached approximately 95% validation accuracy. The more interesting problem was interaction design: confidence thresholds, accidental activation, and feedback mattered as much as raw model performance.

## What I would revisit

A current version would treat gestures as temporal sequences rather than isolated frames and would test recurrent or convolutional sequence models against the original classifier.
